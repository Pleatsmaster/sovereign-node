# AUTO-WORK-0 — Standing Bounded Authority: Internal Analysis

## Status

CONSTITUTIONAL RECORD. Authorized by operator Stephan (chat), 2026-10-01.

This is the first standing authority class for LIFE-0. It does not declare
global autonomy earned. Authority is earned per consequence class.

## The correction this records

think ≠ change self ≠ change world.

Gating all three almost identically produced a sensor attached to a
permission queue: the organism could perceive, but every meaningful
transition terminated at the operator. That is not a living loop.

This grant supersedes the standing rule "no mission launches without an
explicit per-mission operator act" ONLY for missions that mechanically
satisfy every criterion below. All other missions remain under per-mission
human authorization.

This grant modifies no pulse code, no scheduler unit, no frozen record, and
no organism state. The pulse still never launches, never invokes a worker,
never spends. Dispatch under this authority happens outside the pulse, under
this constitutional grant, and is recorded as such.

Relation to the 2026-10-01 "stop touching LIFE-0" directive: that directive
is superseded for this specific operator-authorized constitutional change
only. Unprompted scheduler machinery, TOCTOU refactors, and autonomy
expansion remain out of scope.

## Criteria — ALL must hold

AUTO-WORK-0 may autonomously dispatch a replaceable worker after STAGED
only when ALL hold:

- mission is classified INTERNAL_ANALYSIS
- inputs are already-authorized local/read-only evidence
- no external actuation
- no account creation
- no payment or billing
- no secrets/private-data export
- no capability grant
- no modification of K
- no modification of H
- no modification of M
- no promotion/install/activation of any candidate
- outputs confined to mission workspace / candidate artifacts
- bounded worker budget
- every invocation and artifact recorded
- failure stops locally; no autonomous repair that expands scope

## Chain

clock → sense → need → STAGED → AUTO-WORK-0 classification → worker →
local consequence.

Only after that does a human gate apply, if the result asks to persist,
modify, spend, publish, deploy, or touch the outside world.

## AUTONOMOUS NOW

sense; detect; form bounded needs; select eligible internal work; invoke
worker; read evidence; reason; write local candidate artifacts; record
outcome; fail/refuse.

## STILL HUMAN-GATED

external execution; external writes; financial commitment; private-data
release; new capabilities; promotion; H mutation; M mutation; K mutation;
standing-authority expansion.

## What this does not mean

- Global autonomy is NOT earned. One bounded class, revocable.
- The reporter cron remains read-only. agent may report life ≠ agent
  causes life. Dispatch under AUTO-WORK-0 is performed under this
  constitutional grant by the operator's agent, never by the reporter.
- No external actuation, no persistence, no promotion under this authority.
  A candidate artifact is inert until a human gates its next consequence
  class.
- Classification is mechanical and fail-closed
  (scripts/classify_auto_work_0.py). Ambiguity → INELIGIBLE → the mission
  falls back to per-mission human authorization. No rescue.
