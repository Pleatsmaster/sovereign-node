# RESEARCH_HORIZON_0
STATUS: FROZEN
PURPOSE: Declare the currently open Namariel research horizon for
deterministic opportunity detection.
This document declares questions, not tasks.
A question being OPEN does not authorize work.
A question being referenced by an opportunity does not make that
opportunity valuable.
A proposal remains a candidate until accepted through the existing
commitment-admission boundary.
Only the operator may add, remove, close, reopen, or redefine horizon
questions.
The model may not create new horizon ids.
------------------------------------------------------------
RH0 — CAUSAL PERSISTENCE
STATUS: CLOSED_BASELINE
Question:
Can inherited history causally alter later behavior or transformation?
Disposition:
Established sufficiently for the present program.
RH0 is retained as a baseline and may be referenced by evidence,
but it MUST NOT generate opportunities merely because more evidence
of persistence appears.
------------------------------------------------------------
RH1 — INHERITED LEVERAGE
STATUS: OPEN
Question:
Can an inherited transformation Δ1 improve the quality, reliability,
or cost of producing a later transformation Δ2 under a distinct
subsequent pressure, relative to an appropriate matched control?
This is stronger than persistence.
Required distinction:
Δ1 existing is not enough.
Δ2 occurring is not enough.
Inheritance must causally improve the production of Δ2.
------------------------------------------------------------
RH2 — INHERITED SELECTION
STATUS: OPEN
Question:
Can inherited history increase the probability of selecting a useful
intervention under later conditions, with worker capability and current
evidence appropriately matched?
The causal difference must reside in inherited Namariel state rather
than a stronger worker, prompt injection, or externally supplied answer.
------------------------------------------------------------
RH3 — INHERITED PLASTICITY IN ORDINARY ECOLOGY
STATUS: OPEN_PARTIAL
Established background:
Controlled sequences have shown that inherited history can alter a
later persistent transformation.
Open question:
Does ordinary Namariel operation naturally encounter pressures under
which that inherited difference changes subsequent transformation and
then matters to later interaction?
Do not reopen the already-established controlled-sequence result.
The open object is expression under ordinary ecology.
------------------------------------------------------------
RH4 — ENDOGENOUS CAPABILITY ACQUISITION
STATUS: OPEN
Question:
With the external worker W fixed or appropriately matched, can Namariel
acquire a capability C such that the causal difference responsible for
C resides in organism state S=(H,M), rather than in W or external
scaffolding?
Capability must be absent beforehand under the declared assay.
Acquisition must survive the relevant worker-control test.
------------------------------------------------------------
RH5 — RECURSIVE DEVELOPMENT
STATUS: OPEN
Question:
Does acquiring one capability C1 increase Namariel's subsequent
capacity to acquire a hard-novel capability C2?
The novelty family for C2 must not make the result reducible to trivial
same-family transfer.
Evidence must distinguish:
    C increases
from:
    the capacity to produce later useful C increases.
This is the current recursive-development frontier.
------------------------------------------------------------
HORIZON RULES
1. CLOSED questions do not create opportunities.
2. OPEN questions may anchor opportunities but never authorize work.
3. A result may bear on zero, one, or several horizon questions.
4. Absence of a link means bearing is unresolved, not positive.
5. NO_PROPOSAL remains valid for every observed opportunity.
6. Horizon modification requires an explicit operator event.
7. The proposal mechanism may neither widen this horizon nor reinterpret
   CLOSED as OPEN.
